About Us

A travel company built around the traveller.

Founded in 2019 in Kolkata. Journeys across India — and beyond.

From India, to the world.

Acanthis Journey is a travel company founded in 2019 in Behala, South Kolkata. For six years we have planned and managed journeys across India — and selected international destinations — for different kinds of travellers and budgets — couples, honeymooners, families, groups, friends, senior travellers, solo and private travellers, corporate groups, pilgrimage, leisure, adventure, luxury and budget journeys.

Alongside India, we plan selected international journeys — Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand and Bangkok, Dubai, Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan, Nepal, Bhutan, the Maldives, and further afield when the trip asks for it. Same personal planning. Same 24/7 support.

We don't believe travel should feel like choosing a package from a catalogue. We take the time to understand the traveller before planning the journey.

In that time, the company has grown largely through recommendations. That is the kind of trust we are interested in building — personal attention, thoughtful planning, 24/7 support, and itineraries designed around individual travellers rather than templates.

Founder

Tithi Mondal

Founder · Since 2019

Acanthis Journey was founded by Tithi Mondal in 2019 with a simple belief: travel should feel exciting before the journey begins, comfortable while you're travelling and memorable long after you return.

The company focuses on understanding travellers first and then building journeys around their expectations, preferences and pace.

You tell us what matters. We plan around it.
